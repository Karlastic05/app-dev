# 🎬 My Favorite Series & Movies

Here is a quick overview of the shows and horror movies I enjoy watching!

📺 Favorite Series

1. *The Rookie*
> "Starting over isn't easy, especially for small-town guy John Nolan who, after a life-altering incident, is pursuing his dream of being an LAPD officer."

* **Genre:** Police Procedural / Crime Drama
* **Why I love it:** Great mix of intense police action, humor, and character growth.

2. *Chiikawa*
* **Genre:** Slice of Life / Comedy / Fantasy
* **Why I love it:** Super cute characters navigating everyday challenges, working jobs, and occasionally fighting monsters in a surprisingly relatable world.

👻 Favorite Movies

| Movie Title | Genre | Notable Elements |
| **The Conjuring** | Supernatural Horror | Based on the real-life case files of Ed and Lorraine Warren. |
| **Insidious** | Supernatural Horror | Astral projection, *The Further*, and intense jump scares. |

Top Horror Highlights
1. **Atmosphere & Tension:** Both *The Conjuring* and *Insidious* build dread without relying solely on gore.
2. **Iconic Villains:** From *Bathsheba* and *Valak* to the *Lipstick-Face Demon*.
